# Software Engineer · Solutions Architect | MSc @ Poli-USP

## 🧠 About Me

Computer Engineer focused on designing scalable software systems and modern platforms.

I work primarily with backend systems, distributed architectures and cloud infrastructure, leading the design and development of production systems used by companies and public institutions.

My role often involves acting as a **technical leader and solutions architect**, coordinating development teams, reviewing code, defining architectural decisions and ensuring long-term maintainability of the systems we build.

I apply principles such as **Clean Architecture, Domain-Driven Design and event-driven architectures**, building systems designed to scale and evolve over time.

Currently I coordinate undergraduate technology programs at the **Mauá Institute of Technology** while pursuing my **Master’s degree in Software Engineering at the University of São Paulo (Poli-USP)**.

I am also interested in **building technology products**, applying software engineering and architecture practices to create scalable and sustainable platforms.
